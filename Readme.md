# 🚀 CollExpress — Campus Delivery & Services Platform

**CollExpress** is a college-focused platform designed to make campus life easier by bringing essential services together in one place. From ordering food from campus canteens to renting electrical appliances, CollExpress aims to provide students with a convenient and accessible experience.

## ✨ Features

- 🍔 **Canteen Delivery** — Order food from Yellow Canteen, Green Canteen, and Red Canteen.
- ☕ **Café Delivery** — Enjoy your favorite beverages and snacks from Caffè Da Latte.
- 🔌 **Electrical Rentals** — Rent electrical appliances and equipment when you need them instead of purchasing them.
- 🎓 **Student-Focused Experience** — Designed around the everyday needs of college students.
- 🌐 **Centralized Platform** — Access multiple campus services through one platform.

## 🏪 Available Services

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| Yellow Canteen | Food ordering and campus delivery |
| Green Canteen | Food ordering and campus delivery |
| Red Canteen | Food ordering and campus delivery |
| Caffè Da Latte | Café beverages, snacks, and delivery |
| Rent Electricals | Convenient electrical appliance rentals |

## 🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to simplify everyday college life by making food delivery and essential rental services more convenient for students, all through a single platform.

## 🛠️ Technologies

The project includes an HTML-based website interface. Additional technologies and backend components can be documented as the project develops.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser to view the landing page.

If the project uses PHP or a database, configure a compatible local server and database before running the complete application.
